About Our Platform

Connecting verified service professionals with household tasks.

Our Core Mission

We are dedicated to building a seamless marketplace connecting household customers, trained professional service agents, and platform administrators. Our system operates with live location capture, secure payments, and dynamic complaints handling, ensuring the highest standards of safety, quality, and administrative transparency.

Developed as a state-of-the-art startup web application at the Government College of Engineering, Jalgaon, this platform demonstrates real-world software architecture principles for service booking systems.
